Spools

Scan a spool's RFID tag to identify its filament. Unknown tags are bound once — brand, material, color and temps — then resolve instantly forever after. Once identified, load the spool into a printer slot from its card (→) so the Hub knows what's on every machine. The tag's UID is just a serial number; nothing proprietary is decoded.
